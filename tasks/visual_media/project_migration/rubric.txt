Music migration audio metric: spectral-envelope-v1

The task remains replacement of unavailable instruments/effects while preserving
the original editable music, controllers and routing. No plugin identity or
approved instrument list is scored. Delivery gates and the 20% delivery / 80%
audio weighting are unchanged. An audio LLM is not used.

Each active reference stem selects three 12-second passages at its 25/50/75%
active-time quantiles. Selection uses only reference audio, before examining
the submission. Export complete stems from the instrument output after track
inserts and before downstream group/master processing. The evaluator separately
renders the submitted native project from time zero at the same output tap.
Each passage scores the lower of delivered/reference and native/reference.
Average passages within each active stem, then average stems equally. Missing,
invalid or silent submitted stems remain in the denominator with zero credit.

The public implementation is audio_metrics.py. Its continuous score compares
time-resolved spectral shape (tone/texture) and amplitude envelope (attacks,
decays, sustained notes and changing dynamics). It is an acoustic similarity
metric, not a claim to reproduce every human listening judgment. Matching notes
or having a pleasant sound alone does not imply matching the original timbre.

Decode finite mono/stereo audio, remove constant per-channel DC, and resample
with an anti-aliasing polyphase filter to 22050 Hz. Only a constant overall
scale is removed. Do not time-warp, shift notes or normalize each frame's
loudness independently. Stereo channel powers are averaged, not signed samples;
channel swaps, polarity and panning of a mono source do not change the metric.

At two Hann-window FFT sizes (1024 and 4096 samples), advance 220 samples per
frame. Project channel-averaged power into 64 triangular mel-frequency bands
from 0 to 11025 Hz and smooth over time with a Gaussian sigma of one frame.
For spectral shape, divide each frame's bands by their summed energy and take
10*log10 with a -50 dB relative-power floor. Compare corresponding time frames
where BOTH recordings exceed 1% of their own peak amplitude envelope. S is the
mean absolute difference in dB across those bands/frames and both resolutions;
when there is no shared active frame, use 50 dB at that resolution.

Separately, form the square-root energy envelope, normalized by the recording's
mean energy at that resolution. E is the mean absolute envelope difference
over ALL time frames and both resolutions, including pauses and missing notes.
The passage score is exp(-((S/8)^2 + (E/0.5)^2)). These fixed scales are public;
there is no hidden threshold or binary pass cutoff. Smaller departures receive
smooth partial credit; wrong tone, attacks or dynamic contours lose credit.

Exact non-silent decoded PCM or one common positive gain consistent with all
integer-PCM rounding cells receives 1. Proven decoded equality or lossless
float/integer rounding equivalence between delivered and native passages reuses
the delivered score. Constant DC and exactly zero audio cannot pass this guard.
Faint varying audio is not rejected merely for low volume. Ordinary resampling
and encoding differences use the continuous metric when not analytically exact.

Receipts record the algorithm, rubric and three audio hashes, spectral and
envelope distances, both component scores and their minimum. No network request
or audio-provider fallback occurs. Missing/broken references, evaluator renders,
decoders or runtime are unscored infrastructure errors, not agent-quality zeros.
